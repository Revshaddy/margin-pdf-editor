    How to use Margin

    Open a PDF, make your changes, then press Download PDF. Your file stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

    Open / Merge PDF
    Open replaces the current file. Merge PDF adds another file's pages to the end. You can also drag PDFs onto the window.
    Select
    Click anything you've added to move it. Drag the corner square to resize. Press Delete to remove it, or use the arrow keys to nudge it.
    Edit text
    Click a line of the PDF's own text. It's covered with white and a copy you can edit appears on top. Clear it completely to delete the line.
    Add text
    Click where the text should go and type. Set the font, size and color in the bar above the page.
    Draw · Highlight
    Drag on the page. These tools stay on until you pick another one.
    Whiteout
    Drag a box to hide anything on the page. Combine it with Add text to replace content.
    Box · Ellipse · Line
    Drag to draw a shape.
    Image · Sign
    Pick a picture or draw your signature. It's placed on the current page, ready to move and resize.
    Forms
    Type into the tinted fields and tick the boxes. In the download, your answers are printed onto the page, so the fields are no longer fillable.
    Pages
    In the Pages list, drag to reorder, or use the buttons to rotate, move or delete a page. + inserts a blank page.
    Undo
    Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes (use ⌘ on a Mac).
